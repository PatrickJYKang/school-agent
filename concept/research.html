Primary sources reviewed October 28, 2026

What exists already?

Substantial pieces exist already. The useful new work is a school configuration, a selected-context Canvas experience, a small set of education-specific helpers, and clear student-facing guidance. The reviewed sources do not establish a complete, maintained, turnkey high-school edition.

Read official product documentation, institutional service documentation, GitHub repository metadata, READMEs, and the named StingChat commit. No candidate was installed or run.

Version and verification limits

LibreChat documentation was labeled Latest (dev). The releases collection listed v0.8.9-rc1, published 2026-10-26, and v0.8.8, published 2026-10-01; both were marked prerelease by GitHub. Pin and verify a release before implementation. Documentation is evidence of capability, not a test of a deployment.
